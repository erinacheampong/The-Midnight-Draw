Event readings are approachable and conversational, centered on reflection and connection rather than predictions. A dedicated reading table offers guests a moment to step away and enjoy an individual experience.

How readings fit into your celebration

Guests can join between activities or during a dedicated reading window, with participation always optional. Short readings take approximately five minutes, allowing up to 12 guests per hour per reader. We discuss your expected participation, service hours and any additional readers before confirming your quote.

Ready for your reading hours

We confirm a dedicated reading area, table and chair layout, venue access and arrival time in advance. Setup is coordinated before your booked reading hours, with a two hour minimum for event bookings.
