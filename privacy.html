Privacy Policy

How The Midnight Draw handles information submitted through this website.

Effective August 19, 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through The Midnight Draw website, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, event date and time, event location, event type, estimated guest count, requested reading time, referral source, and details about your event.

Basic technical information may also be processed automatically by the services that host or deliver the website, such as browser type, device information, IP address, or similar connection data.

How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used to respond to your inquiry, discuss availability and event details, communicate about requested services, and maintain reasonable business records related to your request.

Form Processing and Service Providers

The contact form on this website is processed through Formspree. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to that service so your inquiry can be delivered to The Midnight Draw. Service providers may process information according to their own privacy practices.

Sharing of Information

The Midnight Draw does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, receive inquiries, communicate with you, or comply with legal obligations.

Cookies and Tracking

The Midnight Draw does not currently use advertising cookies or targeted advertising tools on this website. Third party services used to host, deliver, or process the website may use standard technical tools necessary to provide their services.

Data Retention

Inquiry information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, manage potential or completed event services, maintain business records, or meet legal obligations. Information that is no longer reasonably needed may be deleted.

Your Choices

You may contact The Midnight Draw to ask what personal information you have submitted, request a correction, or request deletion of information where appropriate. Some records may need to be retained when required for legitimate business or legal purposes.

Security

Reasonable steps are taken to protect information submitted through the website. No online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Links to Other Websites

This website may link to third party websites or services, including Instagram. The Midnight Draw is not responsible for the privacy practices of third party websites.

Children's Privacy

This website and its inquiry form are intended for adults arranging events and are not directed to children. The Midnight Draw does not knowingly request personal information from children through this website.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services, or privacy practices. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Privacy Questions or Requests

If you have a question about this policy or want to make a privacy request, please use the Contact page and identify your message as a privacy request.
