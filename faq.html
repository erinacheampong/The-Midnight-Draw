Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of events can you attend?

Birthdays, bachelorette parties, weddings, corporate gatherings, themed celebrations and other private events.

How long is each reading?

Event readings are typically around five minutes, depending on the guest and the event format.

Do you bring your own setup?

Yes. The experience includes a styled tarot reading table and personal readings. Setup details can be tailored to complement your event.

Where do you travel?

The Midnight Draw serves Southern California. Include your location in the booking form to confirm availability and any travel costs.

How do I reserve a date?

Submit your event information to request availability and a quote. After your event is confirmed, a 50% nonrefundable deposit secures the booking.

When is the remaining balance due?

The remaining balance is due upon arrival on the day of service.
