A Little Clarity Before You Book

No experience is needed. Readings are welcoming and conversational, with room for questions and reflection. Guests can come with a question or simply see what comes up, and participation is always optional.
At approximately five minutes per reading, plan for up to 12 guests per hour per reader, with time for transitions considered in your quote. Share how many guests you hope will participate and your reading hours. Additional readers can be discussed for larger events, subject to availability.
I bring the tarot cards, a styled reading setup, a table and chairs. We will confirm the layout and any venue requirements in advance. Your host or venue provides a designated space where guests can sit comfortably.
Send your date, location, event type, estimated guest count and desired service hours through the inquiry form. I will confirm availability and provide a custom quote. Once the details are confirmed, a 50% nonrefundable deposit reserves your date, with the balance due upon arrival on your event day.
