At Your Event

A seat at the reading table.

A welcoming space, a personal conversation and a little time with the cards. Guests can enjoy a reading as part of the celebration, whether they are tarot curious or already love the practice.

  1. A setting that belongs

    A dedicated reading table with thoughtful details styled to complement your gathering. We discuss the space and setup before your event.

  2. A moment of their own

    Guests who would like a reading take a seat for an approachable, individual conversation. No tarot experience is needed.

  3. A flow that fits

    Reading length, service hours and guest flow are discussed around your event. For larger gatherings, we can explore additional reader options.
